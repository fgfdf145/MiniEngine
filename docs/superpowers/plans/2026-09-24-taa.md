# TAA Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Temporal anti-aliasing: jittered projection plus a compute resolve with reprojection, Catmull-Rom history, variance clipping and luma-weighted blending; TAA off renders today's image.

**Architecture:** Jitter lives only in what the renderer uploads; motion vectors use an unjittered view-projection. A new `Taa` compute pass after Forward writes `SceneTaa`, which the exposure and tone mapping passes read; with TAA off it copies `SceneHdr` through.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, Vulkan compute, GLSL 450, glm, CTest executables.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-taa-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Halton(2, 3), 8 offsets in (-0.5, 0.5) px; `P[2][0] += 2 jx / width`, `P[2][1] += 2 jy / height`.
- `viewProjNoJitter` appended at the end of the camera block.
- `SceneTaa`: storage target, `R16G16B16A16_SFLOAT`. History: two RGBA16F images owned by the pass, `GENERAL` layout.
- Blend 0.1 current; variance clip gamma 1.0 in YCoCg; weights `1 / (1 + exposed luma)`.
- TAA on by default; off or forward-only: no jitter, passthrough copy.

## Review Focus

- A still camera must give zero velocity with jitter on (otherwise the whole image smears).
- History after a resize, a content reload or TAA toggled on: invalid, current only.
- Background pixels under camera rotation must reproject by camera motion, not stay put.
- NaN or infinite HDR values (the sun disk clamps at 65504) must not poison history.
- Light clusters and VBAO must use the same jittered projection as the rasterised depth.
